      How EmoFace works

      1. Tell us about you. Your name, age and gender label the history saved on this computer.
      2. Take or upload a photo. The camera is on one side and the image to analyse appears on the other.
      3. Confirm or retake. Retake goes back to the camera. Confirm sends the photo to the model.
      4. Read the result. EmoFace finds the face, AlexNet scores seven expressions, and the result is saved to your history.

      AlexNet is trained on the FER-2013 dataset. It names the expression on a face. It cannot tell what a person actually feels.
